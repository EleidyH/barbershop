About Us

A neighbourhood barbershop with a simple idea: a good haircut takes time.

Our story

90 Barber Shop started with one chair and a belief that a trip to the barber should be the best half hour of your week. Not rushed, not upsold, just a proper cut from someone who listens.

Today we're a team of barbers who care about the details: clean fades, sharp lines, beards that look intentional, and shaves that leave you feeling new.

Whether it's your first cut or your five-hundredth, you get the same attention and the same welcome.

What we stand for

Craft

Every cut starts with a conversation about what you want, then the technique to deliver it.

Comfort

Good chairs, warm towels and no rush. Book a slot and it's yours.

Community

We're a local shop. Regulars, first-timers and kids in for their first cut are all welcome.

Ready for a fresh cut?

Pick a time that suits you and we'll have a chair ready.
